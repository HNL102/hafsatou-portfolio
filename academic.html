Academic Profile

Connecting scholarship, mentorship, and practical finance.

I advise Master's and doctoral students from early research questions through methodology, analysis, scholarly writing, and thesis defense preparation. My approach supports independent thinking, clear academic communication, and rigorous research practice.

My doctoral research examined the role of financial regulation in SME adoption of fintech solutions in Cameroon. That work informs my continuing interest in financial inclusion, mobile lending, digital finance, and responsible innovation in emerging markets.

I bring a student-centered approach to academic advising, workshop facilitation, research-methods coaching, peer review, and conference participation.
